The Ranjani Difference

Why Thousands of Families Trust Ranjani Farm Shop

We founded Ranjani Farm Shop with one principle: to serve poultry clean enough for our own children to eat with joy and health. Unlike ordinary marketplace stalls where meat sits at ambient room temperature, we follow stringent international food hygiene rules.

Zero Chemical & Preservative Wash

We strictly refuse chlorine washes or artificial coloring. Our chicken retains its natural sweet flavor and nutrition.

Pasture Raised & Healthy Grain Diets

Birds roam in well-ventilated, cruelty-free sheds and eat nutrient-dense corn, soy, and vitamin greens.

Sanitized Stainless Steel Processing

Grade 304 food stainless counters, sterilized knives, and gloved butchers for absolute cleanliness.

Exact Grams Guarantee

You pay for pure meat, not melting ice or heavy plastic weights. Complete transparent billing every time.
